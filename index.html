Common Buyer Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for export? ▼

Our standard export MOQ is 1 × 40ft High Cube (HC) container holding approximately 26.5 Metric Tons (530 compressed bales of 50 kg each). For trial shipments, we can accommodate 1 × 20ft FCL container (~14.0 Metric Tons).

How do you manage moisture control during sea transit? ▼

We ensure all lidi sawit is sun-dried to a verified moisture level of ≤14.0% prior to baling. During container stuffing, we apply heavy-duty container lining, silica gel absorption packets, and ensure proper container ventilation to prevent humidity damage during long ocean voyages.

What are your accepted payment terms? ▼

We typically require a 50% down payment (DP) upon contract signing, with the remaining 50% paid against scanned Bill of Lading (B/L) and export document copies (DP min 50/50). For established buyers, we also accept Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C) at sight from top-tier international banks.

Can we request a physical product sample? ▼

Yes! We provide 2 kg complimentary export samples of both Grade A (110-120cm) and Grade B (80-110cm) lidi sawit. International courier freight costs are borne by the prospective buyer or can be charged via your DHL/FedEx account.
